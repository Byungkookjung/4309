      Excel Activity Export

      Download all income and expenses in your selected period as an Excel (.xlsx) file. Dates, amounts, reasons, details and shared expenses are included.

      Only today or earlier dates can be selected. The end date is not included, so today's unfinished day is excluded when it is selected. Once November ends, choose November 1 to December 1 for November only. The file includes Activity and Summary sheets in CAD, with original and my-share amounts.
